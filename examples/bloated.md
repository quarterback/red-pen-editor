There are several important changes to the permit application process that residents should know about. The city is making changes to the permit application process that will affect how residents apply.

Beginning May 1, residents must submit permit applications online. Paper applications will still be accepted at the library.

The new online application requirement means that residents will need to use the online system to apply for permits starting May 1. Residents who prefer paper can still visit the library.

The city will review applications within 10 business days. Applicants will receive an email when the decision is ready.

In conclusion, these are the important changes residents should be aware of when submitting their permit applications.
